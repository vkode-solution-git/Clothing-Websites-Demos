Product Description

Bring rich colour and elegant shimmer to your celebrations with this Deep Maroon Banarasi Silk Saree from PARIDHAN COLLECTION. Silver zari floral jaal detailing creates a beautiful contrast against the deep maroon base.

The rich zari border frames the floral design, giving the saree a distinctive traditional look. Its maroon and silver combination makes it a beautiful choice for wedding celebrations, receptions, festive gatherings and family occasions.

Style it with coordinated jewellery for an elegant occasion ensemble.

Styling Suggestion

Pair this saree with silver-toned jewellery or pearl accents. Finish with a sleek bun, a delicate bindi and an understated clutch to highlight the floral zari design.

Care Instructions

  • Professional dry cleaning recommended.

  • Store wrapped in soft muslin cloth.

  • Keep away from moisture and prolonged direct sunlight.

  • Refold periodically to prevent lasting creases.

  • Follow the product’s fabric care label when ironing.

  • Avoid spraying perfume directly onto the fabric or zari.
